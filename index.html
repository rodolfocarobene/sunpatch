See where the sun comes into your home

Pick your building on a map, sketch your apartment and its windows, then watch the patches of direct sunlight move across your floor, for any day of the year.

Open the app Try the Milan example

How it works

  1. 1 Find your building

    Search an address and click your building on the OpenStreetMap map. Its neighbours come along, so their shadows count.

  2. 2 Sketch your apartment

    Choose the floor, draw the outline of your flat, and click to add windows and interior walls.

  3. 3 Watch the sun move

    Slide the date and time. The floors above are cut away so you can see the sun patches travel through the rooms.

  4. 4 Walk inside

    Step into first person and look around. Use the keys to move through the day and see when a room gets its light.

What you get

  • Any date, any hour

    Real sun positions for your location, from the winter solstice to midsummer.

  • Real neighbours

    Surrounding buildings come from OpenStreetMap with their heights and cast shadows on yours.

  • Your own plan

    Draw the outline, windows and walls to scale. Fix a missing balcony on the building if the map lacks it.

  • Cutaway and walk mode

    See the apartment from above, or stand in it and look at the window you care about.

  • Export and import

    Download a plan as JSON and load it again later or on another device.

  • No sign-up

    Open the page and start. There is no account, no install and no cost.

What it does and doesn't model

Models

  • Direct sun only: the beam through each window, with shadows from your building and the neighbours.
  • Building heights from OpenStreetMap (height tag, else levels, else a default).
  • Windows with their own width, sill and height.

Does not model

  • Diffuse or reflected light, so shaded rooms look dark.
  • Roof shapes: roofs are flat.
  • Terrain and distant hills: the ground is flat for now.
  • Trees, awnings and window frames.

Treat it as a good estimate, not a survey. OpenStreetMap heights are sometimes missing or wrong.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Your plans are saved in this browser's local storage and never leave your device. There is no account and no tracking. The app only contacts the free map services it needs to show the map and find buildings.
